## CL-EXC-01 Exclusions: intentional acts
We do not pay for loss caused by the deliberate act of the insured or beneficiary, or for loss while committing an unlawful act.
## CL-DOC-02 Documentation required for claims
A claim must be supported by a completed claim form, proof of loss, itemised invoices or repair estimates, and for health claims a discharge summary. Missing documents suspend the claim until received.
## CL-FT-03 Fast-track settlement
Claims at or below the fast-track limit with complete documents and no fraud indicators may be settled without field assessment.
## CL-FRD-04 Suspected fraud
Claims with fraud indicators are referred to the special investigations unit and settlement is held pending investigation.
## CL-NOT-05 Notice of loss
The insured must notify us of a loss within thirty days. Late notice may reduce the amount payable if it prejudices investigation.
## CL-PAY-06 Premium status
Cover is in force only while premium is paid or within the grace period. Claims arising while a policy has lapsed are not payable.
